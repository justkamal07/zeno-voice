Zeno Voice, in about 90 seconds

Five moments: a pickup, a Tanglish intent, a Hinglish request cut off mid-sentence, a status check, and a Tamil evening note that becomes a shareable Zen Card. This walkthrough is recorded from the prototype's demo replay mode: the turns are scripted and the voices are synthesized (Kokoro TTS, an Indian female voice for Zeno and an Indian male voice for Kamal), while every tool call runs through the real ZenMode ledger code. Live mode runs the same loop through the Gemini Live API with your own key, shows first-audio latency, and has a Record live demo button that saves the screen, your voice and Zeno's voice as a video.
